Instructions:  1. All questions are compulsory. Each question carries 1 mark; there is no negative marking, so attempt every question.  2. Each question has four alternatives — exactly one is correct.  3. Mark all answers on the OMR sheet provided at the end by completely darkening the appropriate circle with a blue or black ball pen.  4. Use of calculators or electronic devices is not permitted.
1.A metal rod and a wooden rod of the same size are left in the sun. The metal rod feels much hotter. What does this suggest?
A) Both conduct heat equally wellB) Wood conducts heat better than metalC) Metals are generally better conductors of heat than woodD) Metals absorb no heat at all
2.A liquid in a beaker is heated so that its temperature rises from 27 °C to 77 °C. What is the rise in temperature expressed on the Kelvin scale?
A) 350 KB) 50 KC) 323 KD) 104 K
3.A mango tree has one main woody trunk that branches only well above the ground. How is its habit classified?
A) HerbB) ShrubC) CreeperD) Tree
4.Milk left out of the refrigerator turns sour and curdles, and cannot become fresh milk again. What type of change is this?
A) Not a change at allB) Physical and reversibleC) Chemical and irreversibleD) Physical but irreversible only
5.Riya measures a pencil using a broken ruler that starts at the 2 cm mark. The other end of the pencil reads 14 cm. What is the pencil's actual length?
A) 14 cmB) 16 cmC) 2 cmD) 12 cm
6.An ice cube left on a table slowly turns into a puddle of water. What is this change called?
A) FreezingB) EvaporationC) SublimationD) Melting
7.A person whose diet consists mostly of rice and bread is consuming a diet rich mainly in which nutrient, the body's chief source of energy?
A) VitaminsB) ProteinsC) CarbohydratesD) Minerals
8.After being run through dry hair, a plastic comb picks up small bits of paper. Which force is responsible?
A) Electrostatic forceB) Magnetic forceC) Frictional forceD) Gravitational force
9.The pendulum of a wall clock swings back and forth about a fixed point. What type of motion is this?
A) Circular motionB) Linear motionC) Rotatory motionD) Oscillatory motion
10.A mixture of sand and small stones is separated according to particle size using a mesh. What is this method called?
A) WinnowingB) SublimationC) DecantationD) Sieving
11.A growing child is advised to eat eggs, pulses and milk regularly. Which nutrient do these mainly provide, essential for growth and repair of tissues?
A) VitaminsB) FatsC) CarbohydratesD) Proteins
12.A fern reproduces by spores borne on the underside of its leaves, and has well-developed roots, stems and leaves. To which group does it belong?
A) AngiospermsB) PteridophytaC) ThallophytaD) Bryophyta
13.Two children push a box from opposite sides with equal and opposite forces. What happens to the box?
A) It moves in the direction of the stronger pushB) It does not move, because the forces balance each otherC) It moves in both directions at onceD) Its shape changes permanently
14.An apple falls straight down from a tree instead of floating away. Which force is responsible for this?
A) Gravitational forceB) Magnetic forceC) Frictional forceD) Muscular force
15.The part of a plant that bears leaves, flowers and branches, and generally grows upward towards light, is the:
A) StemB) SepalC) PetalD) Root
16.A goalkeeper catches a fast-moving football and brings it to rest. Which effect of force is shown here?
A) It set the ball into motionB) It stopped a moving objectC) It changed the ball's shape permanentlyD) It increased the ball's speed
17.Ice is heated steadily, but its temperature does not rise while it is melting, even though heat is still being supplied. Where does this heat energy go?
A) It is destroyedB) It is converted into lightC) It is used to overcome the forces holding the particles in place as the state changesD) It escapes without being used at all
18.A balanced diet is one that provides the body with:
A) only carbohydrates and fats in large amountsB) all the necessary nutrients — carbohydrates, proteins, fats, vitamins, minerals, fibre and water — in the right proportionsC) only proteinsD) only water and vitamins
19.A sailor who goes without fresh fruit and vegetables on a long voyage develops bleeding gums, a condition called scurvy. Deficiency of which vitamin causes this?
A) Vitamin KB) Vitamin DC) Vitamin AD) Vitamin C
20.Sand is mixed with water and stirred. The sand remains visible and later settles at the bottom. What type of mixture is this?
A) SolutionB) Heterogeneous mixtureC) Homogeneous mixtureD) Pure compound
21.Which SI unit is correct for measuring the volume of milk in a bottle?
A) SecondB) KilogramC) LitreD) Metre
22.Which of the following correctly describes a key property of the gaseous state?
A) It cannot be compressed at allB) Neither a fixed shape nor a fixed volumeC) No fixed shape but a fixed volumeD) Fixed shape and fixed volume
23.The underground part of a plant that absorbs water and minerals and anchors the plant firmly is the:
A) FlowerB) LeafC) StemD) Root
24.Two students measure the same door and record 198 cm and 199 cm. What does this small difference most likely indicate?
A) It is impossible for two people to get different readingsB) A small reading error by one studentC) The height of the door changedD) The tapes were made of different materials
25.Sanjay pushes hard against a locked door, but it does not budge at all. How much scientific work does he do on the door?
A) Zero, because there is no displacementB) It cannot be determinedC) A large amount of workD) A small amount of work
26.Cooking utensils often have metal bodies but wooden or plastic handles. Why?
A) Wood and plastic are poor conductors of heat, so the handle stays coolB) Metal handles rust very easilyC) To make them lighterD) To make them cheaper to manufacture
27.Muddy river water must be made clear before drinking. Which technique separates the suspended mud particles?
A) FiltrationB) Magnetic separationC) Distillation onlyD) Sublimation
28.A child who rarely gets sunlight develops weak, soft bones, a condition called rickets. Deficiency of which vitamin is the likely cause?
A) Vitamin EB) Vitamin CC) Vitamin DD) Vitamin B
29.Brass is made by mixing copper and zinc in varying proportions, and its properties depend on the ratio used. What type of substance is brass?
A) An elementB) A compoundC) A pure substance with a fixed compositionD) A mixture (alloy)
30.Which of the following is a pure substance rather than a mixture?
A) AirB) LemonadeC) Muddy waterD) Distilled water
31.Farmers separate lighter husk from heavier wheat grains by tossing the mixture in the wind. What is this method called?
A) DecantationB) WinnowingC) SievingD) Sedimentation
32.The green, flat, broad part of a plant that carries out photosynthesis is the:
A) StemB) RootC) FlowerD) Leaf
33.Baking soda mixed with vinegar fizzes and produces a gas, and the original substances cannot be recovered. What type of change is this?
A) Physical changeB) A change of state onlyC) No change has occurredD) Chemical change
34.Rohit kicks a stationary football and it starts rolling across the ground. Which effect of force is shown here?
A) It set a stationary object into motionB) It stopped a moving objectC) It changed the shape of the ballD) It had no effect at all
35.Two bodies of masses in the ratio 2 : 3 are raised to heights above the ground in the ratio 3 : 4. What is the ratio of their gravitational potential energies?
A) 1 : 2B) 2 : 3C) 3 : 4D) 8 : 9
36.Which nutrient provides the most concentrated energy per gram, more than carbohydrates or proteins?
A) FatsB) MineralsC) WaterD) Vitamins
37.In a flower, the female reproductive part, made up of the ovary, style and stigma, is the:
A) PetalB) StamenC) SepalD) Pistil (carpel)
38.In a flower, the male reproductive part that produces pollen grains is the:
A) SepalB) PistilC) PetalD) Stamen
39.A steel almirah keeps the same shape and size no matter which room it is placed in. Which state of matter does this best illustrate?
A) GasB) SolidC) LiquidD) Plasma
40.A child on a merry-go-round moves in a circular path around a fixed centre. What type of motion is this?
A) Oscillatory motionB) Circular motionC) Rectilinear motionD) Random motion
41.Two bodies A and B have masses in the ratio 1 : 2 and are moving with velocities in the ratio 2 : 1. What is the ratio of their kinetic energies?
A) 1 : 1B) 4 : 1C) 1 : 2D) 2 : 1
42.A rose bush has a hard, woody stem with several branches arising close to the ground, and is shorter than a tree. How is it classified?
A) HerbB) CreeperC) TreeD) Shrub
43.Which instrument is used to measure the temperature of the human body?
A) AnemometerB) HygrometerC) Clinical thermometerD) Barometer
44.A porter lifts a 20 kg suitcase through a vertical height of 1 m onto a trolley. Taking g = 10 m/s², how much work does he do against gravity?
A) 2000 JB) 200 JC) 2 JD) 20 J
45.Priya needs to measure a few millilitres of medicine accurately. Which instrument should she use?
A) BucketB) Metre scaleC) Bathroom scaleD) Measuring cylinder
46.In a hydroelectric power station, falling water turns a turbine which generates electricity. What is the correct sequence of energy conversion?
A) Electrical → Potential → KineticB) Chemical → Potential → ElectricalC) Kinetic → Electrical → PotentialD) Potential → Kinetic → Electrical
47.A mixture of iron filings and sulphur powder needs to be separated. Which is the best method?
A) SublimationB) EvaporationC) FiltrationD) Using a magnet
48.Burning paper turns it into ash and smoke. Can this change be reversed?
A) No, but it is only a physical changeB) Yes, easilyC) No, it is an irreversible chemical change in which new substances are formedD) Yes, by cooling the ash
49.A weightlifter lifts a barbell off the ground using his arm muscles. Which type of force is being applied?
A) Electrostatic forceB) Muscular forceC) Frictional forceD) Magnetic force
50.After pollination and fertilisation, the ovary of a flower develops into a fruit, while the ovules develop into:
A) SepalsB) RootsC) PetalsD) Seeds
51.The outermost green, leaf-like parts that protect a flower bud before it opens are the:
A) PetalsB) StamensC) CarpelsD) Sepals
52.A block needs a much bigger push to move across a rough floor than across a smooth one. Which force explains this difference?
A) Frictional forceB) Magnetic forceC) Gravitational forceD) Electrostatic force
53.A pine tree produces seeds that are not enclosed inside any fruit. To which group of plants does it belong?
A) ThallophytesB) AngiospermsC) GymnospermsD) Bryophytes
54.Which of the following is an element, made up of only one type of atom?
A) OxygenB) SaltC) WaterD) Air
55.Ice melts into water on heating, and the water can be frozen back into ice. What type of change is this?
A) Chemical, because a new substance formsB) Physical and reversible, because no new substance is formedC) An irreversible changeD) Both physical and chemical equally
56.Woollen clothes keep us warm in winter mainly because they:
A) generate heat on their ownB) reflect all heat away from the bodyC) trap air, a poor conductor, close to the bodyD) absorb heat from the surroundings and pass it to the body
57.The veins of a leaf branch out in a net-like pattern rather than running parallel. What is this called?
A) Reticulate venationB) Radial venationC) Parallel venationD) Linear venation
58.A sheet of paper is torn into small pieces. Can it be restored to a single sheet, and what type of change is it?
A) No, because it is an irreversible chemical changeB) Yes, because it is a chemical changeC) Yes, and it is a chemical changeD) No, but it is still a physical change since no new substance is formed
59.The Law of Conservation of Energy states that energy:
A) can neither be created nor destroyed, only converted from one form to anotherB) can be destroyed but not createdC) is always lost completely over timeD) can be created but not destroyed
60.Salt needs to be recovered from salt water, and the water need not be collected. Which method should be used?
A) EvaporationB) WinnowingC) FiltrationD) Magnetic separation
61.Squeezing a rubber ball changes its shape temporarily, and it returns to normal when released. Which effect of force does this show?
A) Force can only start motionB) Force can only stop motionC) Force can change the shape of an objectD) Force has no effect on shape
62.Touching a metal spoon that has been left standing in hot tea, Riya finds the handle is warm. How did heat travel to the handle?
A) ConvectionB) RadiationC) ConductionD) Evaporation
63.Water in a pot heats evenly because hot water rises while cooler water sinks to replace it. Which mode of heat transfer is this?
A) InsulationB) ConductionC) ConvectionD) Radiation
64.Smoke particles released into still air are seen to move haphazardly in all directions. What is this type of motion called?
A) Random motionB) Rotatory motionC) Periodic motionD) Rectilinear motion
65.Wet clothes hung out in the sun become dry after a few hours. Which change is chiefly responsible?
A) MeltingB) FreezingC) CondensationD) Evaporation
66.A car travels straight down a highway without turning. What is this type of motion called?
A) Rectilinear motionB) Rotatory motionC) Periodic motionD) Curvilinear motion
67.A person finds it difficult to see clearly in dim light, a condition called night blindness. Deficiency of which vitamin commonly causes this?
A) Vitamin CB) Vitamin DC) Vitamin AD) Vitamin B
68.Comparing the particles of a solid, a liquid and a gas of the same substance, which state has the most loosely packed, freely moving particles?
A) LiquidB) All three are packed equallyC) GasD) Solid
69.Naphthalene balls kept in a cupboard shrink and disappear over several weeks, without ever forming a liquid. Which process explains this?
A) SublimationB) MeltingC) CondensationD) Evaporation
70.Water droplets form on the outside of a chilled glass on a humid day. What causes this?
A) Evaporation of water from inside the glassB) Condensation of water vapour present in the airC) Melting of the glassD) Sublimation of the glass surface
71.Two forces acting on two different bodies are in the ratio 3 : 2, and the displacements produced in the direction of the forces are in the ratio 2 : 5. What is the ratio of the work done by the two forces?
A) 6 : 5B) 5 : 3C) 15 : 4D) 3 : 5
72.Salt dissolves completely in water to form a clear solution with no visible salt particles. What type of mixture is this?
A) SuspensionB) Heterogeneous mixtureC) Homogeneous mixtureD) Compound
73.The key of a toy car is wound up, storing energy in its spring, before the car is released to move. What form of energy was stored in the wound spring?
A) Kinetic energyB) Sound energyC) Chemical energyD) Potential energy
74.A diet lacking fibre-rich foods such as fruits, vegetables and whole grains commonly leads to which digestive problem?
A) ConstipationB) Night blindnessC) RicketsD) Scurvy
75.The colourful, often fragrant parts of a flower that attract insects for pollination are the:
A) PetalsB) SepalsC) AnthersD) Ovary
76.Arjun wants to measure the exact time a marble takes to roll down a ramp. Which instrument should he use?
A) SundialB) CalendarC) StopwatchD) Wall clock
77.Sugar dissolved in tea cannot be seen once dissolved, and every sip tastes equally sweet. This shows that the solution is:
A) A suspensionB) HeterogeneousC) HomogeneousD) A compound
78.Motion that repeats itself after equal intervals of time, such as a swinging pendulum or the Earth's revolution, is called:
A) Periodic motionB) Random motionC) Uniform motion onlyD) Non-uniform motion
79.We feel the warmth of a bonfire from a few metres away, without touching it and without moving air. Which mode of heat transfer explains this?
A) ConductionB) RadiationC) ConvectionD) Diffusion
80.A cyclist covers equal distances in equal intervals of time on a straight, flat road. What type of motion is this?
A) Uniform motionB) Oscillatory motionC) Non-uniform motionD) Random motion
81.A spinning top rotates rapidly about its own fixed axis while staying almost in one spot. What type of motion is this?
A) Rotatory motionB) Oscillatory motionC) Circular motion around another objectD) Translatory motion
82.A wheat plant has many thin roots of similar size, with no single main root. What type of root system is this?
A) Adventitious root systemB) Prop root systemC) Fibrous root systemD) Tap root system
83.Meera measures the length of her study table with a metre scale and records it as 90 cm. What is this length in metres?
A) 0.9 mB) 9 mC) 90 mD) 900 m
84.A boy carries a bucket of water 10 m across level ground without lifting it any higher. What is the work done against gravity?
A) It cannot be found without knowing the bucket's shapeB) It depends on his walking speedC) Zero, because there is no vertical displacementD) The weight of the bucket multiplied by 10 m
85.The smallest particle of an element that can take part in a chemical reaction is called a/an:
A) MoleculeB) MixtureC) CompoundD) Atom
86.A pumpkin plant spreads its weak stem along the ground rather than standing upright. What is such a plant called?
A) ShrubB) CreeperC) TreeD) Climber
87.A mixture of camphor and sand must be separated. Camphor turns directly into vapour on heating. Which method should be used?
A) SublimationB) Magnetic separationC) EvaporationD) Filtration
88.Iron left in humid air slowly develops a reddish-brown coating of rust. What type of change is rusting?
A) Physical and reversibleB) Temporary, since no new substance is formedC) Chemical and irreversible, because a new substance is formedD) A change of state only
89.A rubber band is stretched and then released, returning to its original shape. What type of change is this?
A) Physical and reversibleB) Physical but irreversibleC) Chemical and reversibleD) Chemical and irreversible
90.Water is made of hydrogen and oxygen combined in a fixed ratio, and cannot be separated by simple physical means. Water is therefore an example of:
A) a solutionB) an elementC) a mixtureD) a compound
91.Which of the following is an example of a purely physical change?
A) Burning woodB) Digestion of foodC) Melting of waxD) Rusting of iron
92.A mango sapling, when uprooted, shows one thick main root with thinner roots branching from it. What type of root system is this?
A) Aerial root systemB) Tap root systemC) Stilt root systemD) Fibrous root system
93.A grape vine climbs up a support using tendrils that coil around it. What type of plant is this?
A) HerbB) CreeperC) ClimberD) Tree
94.A study lamp lights up and its bulb feels slightly warm after some time. Into which forms of energy is the electrical energy mainly converted?
A) Light energy and heat energyB) Only sound energyC) Only kinetic energyD) Only chemical energy
95.A ball thrown straight upward slows down while rising and speeds up while falling. Is this uniform or non-uniform motion?
A) Uniform, because gravity is constantB) Non-uniform, because its speed keeps changingC) Uniform, because it returns to the same spotD) It cannot be determined
96.A plant has leaves with parallel venation and a fibrous root system. Which type of plant is it most likely to be?
A) BryophyteB) MonocotC) DicotD) Gymnosperm
97.Pure water is to be obtained from sea water by evaporating it and then condensing the vapour. Which method is this?
A) SievingB) FiltrationC) WinnowingD) Distillation
98.After sand has settled at the bottom of a jar of muddy water, the clear water above is carefully poured off. What is this process called?
A) FiltrationB) DecantationC) EvaporationD) Sublimation
99.On which temperature scale does water freeze at 0° and boil at 100° under normal atmospheric pressure?
A) Fahrenheit scaleB) Kelvin scaleC) Celsius scaleD) Richter scale
100.A magnet attracts an iron nail lying a few centimetres away, without touching it. What type of force is this?
A) Frictional forceB) Muscular forceC) Non-contact (magnetic) forceD) Contact force
