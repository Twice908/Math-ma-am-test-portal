Complex Numbers — Concept Check

Foundations through Cube Roots of Unity · MSBSHSE Std XI, Ch. 1

Q1Definition
Write the complex number $-3 + \sqrt{-16}$ in the standard form $a+ib$.
Q2Conjugate
Find the conjugate of $z = 4 - 5i$, and write the value of $z \cdot \bar z$.
Q3Properties of Conjugate
State whether true or false, with one line of reasoning: "The conjugate of $z_1+z_2$ equals $\bar z_1 + \bar z_2$."
Q4Modulus
Find the modulus $|z|$ of $z = 3 - 4i$.
Q5Argument (Amplitude)
Find the amplitude (argument) of $z = 1 + i$.
Q6Polar Form
Express $z = 1 + i\sqrt3$ in polar form $r(\cos\theta + i\sin\theta)$.
Q7Exponential Form
Write $z = 2\left(\cos\dfrac{\pi}{3} + i\sin\dfrac{\pi}{3}\right)$ in exponential form $re^{i\theta}$.
Q8Argand Diagram
Plot $z = -2 + 3i$ on the Argand diagram (describe the point's position — which quadrant, and its coordinates).
Q9Addition & Subtraction
If $z_1 = 2+3i$ and $z_2 = 1-4i$, find $z_1+z_2$ and $z_1-z_2$.
Q10Multiplication
Find the product $(2+i)(3-2i)$.
Q11Division
Simplify $\dfrac{3+2i}{1-i}$ by rationalising the denominator.
Q12Prove That — Modulus/Conjugate
Prove that: $z \cdot \bar z = |z|^2$ for any complex number $z = a+ib$.
Q13Prove That — Cube Roots of Unity
If $\omega$ is a complex cube root of unity, prove that: $1+\omega+\omega^2 = 0$.
Q14Prove That — Cube Roots of Unity
If $\omega$ is a complex cube root of unity, prove that: $(2-\omega)(2-\omega^2) = 7$.
