Section BVery Short Answers

11. Hydrocarbons are organic compounds containing only carbon and hydrogen atoms.

12. Alkenes have the general formula $C_nH_{2n}$.

13. Aromaticity is the special stability of cyclic, planar, conjugated compounds containing $(4n+2)\pi$ electrons, such as benzene.

14. $CH_3-CH_2-CH=CH_2$ is but-1-ene.

15. Complete hydrogenation of ethyne gives ethane: $C_2H_2+2H_2\xrightarrow{Ni}C_2H_6$.

Section CNamed Reactions

16. Wurtz: $2CH_3Br+2Na\xrightarrow{dry\ ether}CH_3-CH_3+2NaBr$.

17. Kolbe: $2CH_3COONa+2H_2O\xrightarrow{electrolysis}C_2H_6+2CO_2+H_2+2NaOH$.

18. Friedel–Crafts alkylation: $C_6H_6+CH_3Cl\xrightarrow{anhyd.\ AlCl_3}C_6H_5CH_3+HCl$ (toluene).

19. Friedel–Crafts acylation: $C_6H_6+CH_3COCl\xrightarrow{anhyd.\ AlCl_3}C_6H_5COCH_3+HCl$ (acetophenone).

20. Decarboxylation: $CH_3COONa+NaOH\xrightarrow{CaO,\Delta}CH_4+Na_2CO_3$.

Section DShort Answers

21. Markovnikov's rule states that in addition of HX to an unsymmetrical alkene, H attaches to the carbon already having more H atoms. Thus $CH_3CH=CH_2+HBr\to CH_3CHBrCH_3$, giving 2-bromopropane.

22. Ethene is prepared by dehydration of ethanol with concentrated $H_2SO_4$ at about 443 K: $CH_3CH_2OH\xrightarrow[443K]{conc.\ H_2SO_4}CH_2=CH_2+H_2O$.

23. Benzene undergoes electrophilic substitution with nitrating mixture: $C_6H_6+HNO_3\xrightarrow{conc.\ H_2SO_4}C_6H_5NO_2+H_2O$. The electrophile is $NO_2^+$.

Section ELong Answers

24. Calcium carbide reacts with water: $CaC_2+2H_2O\to C_2H_2+Ca(OH)_2$. Ethyne gives silver acetylide with ammoniacal $AgNO_3$: $HC\equiv CH+2AgNO_3+2NH_4OH\to AgC\equiv CAg\downarrow+2NH_4NO_3+2H_2O$. The product is an insoluble silver acetylide precipitate.

25. Without peroxide, propene and HBr give 2-bromopropane through a stable secondary carbocation by Markovnikov addition. With peroxide, HBr gives 1-bromopropane by the Anti-Markovnikov or Kharasch peroxide effect.
