Teacher’s answer key and brief explanations
  1. Q1: B — Only the shown vertical line divides this drawn pentagon into matching halves.
  2. Q2: C — A square has two midlines and two diagonals as symmetry lines.
  3. Q3: B — A non-square rectangle has one horizontal and one vertical line of symmetry.
  4. Q4: C — Each vertex-to-opposite-side midpoint line is a symmetry line.
  5. Q5: A — A scalene triangle has no equal sides and no mirror line.
  6. Q6: D — Every diameter divides a circle into two matching halves; there are infinitely many diameters.
  7. Q7: B — Only the line perpendicular to the diameter through its midpoint is a symmetry line.
  8. Q8: C — The two diagonals of a rhombus bisect it into mirror-image halves.
  9. Q9: B — The vertical line divides the heart into matching left and right halves.
  10. Q10: A — The upper and lower parts are mirror images across its horizontal centre line.
  11. Q11: B — A standard block A has matching left and right sloping sides.
  12. Q12: D — A standard block F has neither horizontal nor vertical mirror symmetry.
  13. Q13: C — A block H is symmetric about both its vertical and horizontal centre lines.
  14. Q14: D — A geometrically perfect circular O has every diameter as a symmetry line.
  15. Q15: B — The left and right wings mirror each other across the vertical body line.
  16. Q16: A — The rectangle is wider than it is tall; its vertical centre line gives matching halves.
  17. Q17: B — For a non-square rectangle, diagonals do not divide it into mirror-image halves.
  18. Q18: C — A non-equilateral isosceles triangle has exactly one symmetry line.
  19. Q19: C — A regular polygon has as many symmetry lines as it has sides.
  20. Q20: D — A regular hexagon has six symmetry lines: three through opposite vertices and three through opposite side midpoints.
  21. Q21: B — A symmetry line creates two halves that match by reflection.
  22. Q22: C — The two diagonals intersect at the centre and form four triangular regions.
  23. Q23: A — A symmetry fold makes corresponding points and edges coincide.
  24. Q24: A — The conventional heart symbol has one vertical mirror line.
  25. Q25: C — Its horizontal and vertical centre lines are the only symmetry lines.
  26. Q26: B — Under the stated restriction, count only the vertical and horizontal midlines.
  27. Q27: B — A square has four symmetry lines.
  28. Q28: C — Reflection preserves perpendicular distance and places the image on the opposite side.
  29. Q29: A — A reflection places corresponding points at equal distances on opposite sides of the mirror line.
  30. Q30: C — A scalene triangle has no mirror line.
  31. Q31: C — A regular polygon has one symmetry line associated with each side/vertex arrangement; a regular octagon has eight.
  32. Q32: B — A square has rotational symmetry of order 4, so a 90° turn maps it onto itself.
  33. Q33: B — Reflection flips a figure across a line to create its mirror image.
  34. Q34: A — Each symmetry line of an equilateral triangle joins a vertex to the opposite side's midpoint.
  35. Q35: C — Three lines join pairs of opposite vertices; the other three pass through opposite side midpoints.
  36. Q36: B — A non-square rectangle and a non-square rhombus each have two symmetry lines.
  37. Q37: A — The heart has a vertical mirror line but not a horizontal one.
  38. Q38: C — Eight equally spaced identical petals give eight reflection axes.
  39. Q39: C — The fold maps the two corners not on the fold onto each other.
  40. Q40: B — The left and right vertical sides coincide under this fold.
  41. Q41: C — A regular n-sided polygon has n lines of symmetry.
  42. Q42: D — A general slanted parallelogram has no reflection symmetry.
  43. Q43: C — A standard block H mirrors across both its horizontal and vertical centre lines.
  44. Q44: D — Reflecting across the centre line maps column 1 to column 4 in the same row.
  45. Q45: B — An equilateral triangle has three symmetry lines.
  46. Q46: B — The line acts as the mirror line for a reflection.
  47. Q47: A — The halves match exactly after reflection; their orientation is reversed.
  48. Q48: C — Every diameter of a circle is a line of symmetry.
  49. Q49: A — A reflection across a symmetry line leaves the whole figure unchanged.
  50. Q50: B — A standard block C is symmetric about a horizontal centre line.
